Sopana · sopana-dte25

Ticks every five minutes from 06:00–13:59 PT. Reads spot, VIX, its own IV rank and a moving average, then marks open positions.

Two IV ranks will disagree. Sopana computes one from its own history; the scanner now computes another. Decide which is authoritative before the veto lands.

Composite vote decides bull put, bear call, or — when votes fail to clear — an iron condor.

Sopana already solved the condor problem — and the scanner has now copied it. No directional conviction produces a condor rather than standing aside. As of 4 August the scanner's directional gate filters verticals only, so its condor path is reached on the same reasoning: both systems now treat “no view, both tails quiet” as a setup rather than a disqualification.

▼ JOIN — veto only, never a trigger ▼

Three rules that make this safe

  • Veto only. It may block a side or an entry. It may never open a trade Sopana would not have opened anyway — so the worst case is fewer trades, not a new failure mode.
  • Fail open. Scanner down or row stale ⇒ no veto. A missing scanner means Sopana trades exactly as it does today, which is a known quantity. Fail-closed would let an unrelated outage silently halt the book.
  • Log before binding. Two weeks non-binding: count every veto and what the trade it would have blocked went on to do.
