Synthesis aggregation versus the weighted mean

running no end date

Synthesis collapses the corpus to distinct stories and spends one Opus call per cycle on the top 40. Only it produces the per-side tail estimates the spread gates depend on, so reverting to mean would disable the tail-risk logic entirely.

Available freethe mean aggregator still runs every cycle — it supplies the sentiment and macro sub-scores in every alert. A comparison exists in the data already; nothing records it.
