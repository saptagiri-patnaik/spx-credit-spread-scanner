Pipeline Architecture

SPX Scanner: from RSS feed to credit spread

Every 45 minutes the scheduler runs one full pass — six collectors, a local LLM scoring each new item, a two-channel directional blend, then an exhaustive scan of every vertical in the 20–25 DTE window. Three independent gates decide whether any of it becomes a trade.

Interval 45 min Cycle time ~14 min Underlying SPX Horizon 6 days Model llama3.1:8b
I/O & external Decision gate Bullish path Bearish path

Scoring — where the 14 minutes goes

Each unscored item is chunked and sent to the local model as strict-JSON prompts. An item with a long article body costs up to three inference calls, which is why this stage dominates the cycle while collection finishes in under a second.

04

The direction decision

Scored items are split into two independent channels by source type, each weighted four ways, then blended. Market trend is folded in last, and confidence is computed separately from direction — a strong direction with thin coverage still fails the gate.

Confidence terms

magnitude = min(1, |direction| / 0.6) — conviction, rescaled: a blended avg rarely exceeds ~0.5 coverage = min(1, num_items / 20) — saturates at 20 scored items agreement = 1 − min(1, |macro − sentiment|) — penalty when the two channels disagree confidence = 0.60·magnitude + 0.20·coverage + 0.20·agreement
Failure mode trend_score falls back to 0.0 when the Schwab token is stale — and 0.85·blend + 0.15·trend applies unconditionally. A missing trend therefore shrinks direction by 15% toward neutral rather than being excluded, which also depresses confidence. Compare the macro/sentiment path, which reweights to 100% when one channel is empty.
05

Finding the best credit spread

Rather than forcing a trade, the scanner enumerates every valid vertical across every expiry in the window — each qualifying short leg paired against every qualifying long leg — then ranks the whole set by a single edge score.

Edge score

ev_ratio = POP · RoR − (1 − POP) — expected return per $1 of risk align = direction, sign-flipped for call spreads — agreement with the directional read edge = ev_ratio + 0.15 · align · confidence — reward spreads that agree with the thesis + 0.05 · min(buffer, 2.0) — reward strikes further beyond the expected move

The alignment term is what makes this directional rather than a pure premium harvest: two spreads with identical economics rank differently depending on whether they lean with the week's read, scaled by how much the model trusts that read.

06

Three gates before a trade is called

A ranked candidate is not a recommendation. All three must pass, or the alert downgrades from TRADE SIGNAL to SPX OUTLOOK and stays in the log rather than going out to Telegram or Discord.

Gate 1 · Timing Market open Regular hours only, Mon–Fri 09:30–16:00 ET. Holidays are not handled — this is why every overnight cycle reports Market: CLOSED.
Gate 2 · Conviction confidence ≥ 0.65 Applied earlier, during candidate generation. Below the gate, or on a NEUTRAL label, zero candidates are produced at all.
Gate 3 · Edge edge ≥ 0.05 The top-ranked candidate must clear the threshold. A best-of-a-bad-set spread is surfaced as a candidate but never recommended.
Alert routing alert_only_on_trade=true means external push happens only when all three gates pass. Everything else is written to logs/spx_scanner.log and the predictions table, so a quiet phone is the expected state, not a failure.
